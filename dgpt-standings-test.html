World Standings Points Breakdown 

  • • Top 28 MPO players and top 18 FPO players qualify* for the Tour Championship at the end of the season.
  • • All competitors can count up to 15 finishes towards their season points totals.
  • • 11 out of 17 DGPT Events (DGPT, DGPT+) (maximum of 3 DGPT events in Europe can count)
  • • 2 out of 2 DGPT Playoffs (must qualify)
  • • Top 2 PDGA Pro Major finishes (out of 3 for MPO and 4 for FPO)

DGPT win = 150 points | DGPT+ win = 200 points | Playoff win = 250 points | PDGA Pro Major win = 300 points

*The top 4 MPO finishers and the top 2 FPO finishers at the MVP Open who are not already qualified via the DGPT World Standings will also qualify for the DGPT Championship. An additional path to qualifying will be offered to winners of DGPT and PDGA Pro Major Events who do not otherwise qualify for the DGPT Championship via the DGPT World Standings. These players will be added to the event as the bottom seeds.

See a full breakdown of the 2025 DGPT World Standings and the 2025 DGPT Europe** Standings here.

**DGPT Europe will maintain its own points standings in 2025, European DGPT & Major events will count towards both the World Standings and DGPT Europe Standings. European Q-Series events will only count towards the DGPT Europe Standings.

***Kristin Lätt has withdrawn from the DGPT Championship. Due to Kristin Lätt’s withdrawal, the Tour Championship cutline has moved from position 18 to position 19.
